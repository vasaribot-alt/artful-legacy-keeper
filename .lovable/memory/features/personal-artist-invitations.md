## Jan's letter to Sonia (as written by Jan, shared 2026-10-12)

> Dear Sonia,
>
> It was a pleasure talking with you at the IAA-Europe symposium in Malmö yesterday. I wanted to follow up personally and give you a clearer picture of the foundation I have established, the Global Artist Registry Foundation — GARF.
>
> I am Norwegian and I have been working with authentication issues in London for many years. The foundation is registered in the Netherlands as a non-commercial stichting (Dutch for Foundation). Its purpose is simple: to help artists keep control of their own documentation and preserve it for at least one hundred years, including beyond their lifetime.
>
> So much of an artist's history is scattered across gallery systems, museum records, old websites and paid storage accounts. Each may hold an important part of the story, but there is often no complete archive under the artist's own control. When a website closes or payments stop, that documentation can become inaccessible or disappear.
>
> GARF brings these records together around the artist: works, photographs, exhibition histories, publications and the documentation that gives them context. Artists retain ownership of their material, decide what is public or private, and can name heirs to take responsibility for their archive in the future. Registration and the core artist archive are free, and the foundation maintains the platform.
>
> If you wish to build a complete catalogue of your works, the natural route is that you yourself ask the galleries and institutions where you have exhibited for extracts of the documentation they hold about you and your artworks — exhibition records, catalogue entries, press material and photographs. GARF can then help you collect those extracts into one archive that you own and control. Their own archives stay untouched; nothing is removed from them and no rights are transferred.
>
> Here is a short presentation explaining the thinking behind the foundation:
> https://globalartistregistry.org/GARF_Why_GARF_Matters.pdf
> And a little more about us: https://globalartistregistry.org/about
>
> No action is needed — this is simply the information I promised to send. If anything here raises questions, I am of course happy to answer them.
>
> Thank you again for the conversation in Malmö. I hope we can keep in touch.
>
> Warm regards, Jan
