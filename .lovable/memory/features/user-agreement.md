- Public /terms page (User Agreement), plain-language draft v0.1 (Oct 2026), marked for legal review before it becomes binding. Linked from the footers of Index, About, FAQ and from a required acceptance checkbox on /register.
- Covers: artist ownership/control of records (edit, export, delete; nothing added to a catalogue without artist approval), free-for-artists non-commercial guarantee (Dutch stichting charter), responsible-use rules, 100-year preservation and succession (named heirs; public estate custodianship; on dissolution archives transfer to a like-minded non-profit, never sold), GDPR privacy, change policy.
- Standing rule: future changes to the terms may never weaken sections 2 (ownership), 3 (non-commercial), and 5 (preservation/succession).
