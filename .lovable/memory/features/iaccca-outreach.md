- All 58 member collections of IACCCA (International Association of Corporate Collections of Contemporary Art, 19 countries) are loaded into `alliance_outreach_targets` with `category = 'corporate_collections'` and `tag = 'IACCCA'`.
- The association itself is a separate target with `category = 'organisations'`, same tag, approached in parallel with its members.
- Ask (approved 2026-10-01): the museum-model ask, NOT the donation ask. `generate-outreach-email` routes `tag = 'IACCCA'` corporate collections to `iaccca_collections` guidance, which forbids the €5-per-work / supporter-tier ask.
- Approved closing text, kept essentially verbatim before the sign-off:
  "We would like to contact artists that are in important collections, so our request is simple: Would you be happy to share the names of the artists in your collection and roughly how many works you hold of each. If you also have the contact details for some of the artists, it would be great if you could share it with us so we can approach them and suggest that they start their complete catalogue. If the number of artists is long, you could also just share with us the ones you think would benefit with a complete catalogue of all works.
  You will of course have free access to the archives of the artists you have shared and who have registered with us."
  (Jan's draft said "Those who share will of course have free access to the archives of the artists you have shared"; pronoun mix fixed to "You will of course have free access…" — flag if Jan wants his original.)
- Thank-you access is SCOPED: a collection sees only the archives of artists from its own collection who have registered. Collection-scoped access permission model must be designed/built before any IACCCA sends.
- The "What we are asking — and what we are not asking" clarity section is still mandatory in IACCCA emails.
- Contact people and websites must be filled per target before sending; never guess emails.
