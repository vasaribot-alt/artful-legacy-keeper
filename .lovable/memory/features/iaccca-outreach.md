name: IACCCA Corporate Collections Outreach
description: The 58 IACCCA member collections are outreach targets tagged IACCCA in corporate_collections, plus the association itself under organisations
type: feature
---
- All 58 member collections of IACCCA (International Association of Corporate Collections of Contemporary Art, 19 countries) are loaded into `alliance_outreach_targets` with `category = 'corporate_collections'` and `tag = 'IACCCA'`.
- The association itself is a separate target with `category = 'organisations'`, same tag, approached in parallel with its members.
- The `corporate_collections` guidance in `generate-outreach-email` makes two asks: awareness (free lifetime registration for their artists) and support (Supporting Collection: donation, endorsement, or joint documentation pilot).
- Mandatory "What we are asking - and what we are not asking" section: records stay where they are, no collection data requested, artists own their archive, GARF is not a marketplace.
- Contact people and websites are not researched yet; they must be filled per target before sending.
