- `patron_tier3` (23): large-scale and blue-chip patrons, digital art patrons, regional leaders

Würth Collection (category corporate_collections, contacted) counts as the first main sponsor conversation.

Framing that works with patrons (never a software pitch):
1. Cultural infrastructure with a 100-year horizon
2. Artist visibility and attribution
3. Global art ecosystem transparency
4. Research and provenance support
5. Discovery of emerging artists

Asks, in order of preference: main sponsorship (named, multi-year), then endorsement or advisory-board support, then encouraging the artists in their collection to register (free for life). Never ask a patron to replace their collection management system, and never include bank details in a first letter.
