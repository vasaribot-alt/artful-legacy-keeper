Partner: Anna Cherevko, Head of Operations at UFDA / Digital Original. Project: 15-artist pilot for displaced Ukrainian artists — GARF leads grant coordination, Stichting Kastanje supports outreach in the Netherlands, UFDA contributes digitisation. Brief and answers sent 2026-10-08; UFDA replied 2026-10-13.

Anna's reply 2026-10-13 — positive ("genuine potential"), but five clarification areas before confirming participation:

1. **Artist pool and selection** — asks whether GARF or Stichting Kastanje already has a preliminary pool: where artists are currently based; whether their physical works and documentation are with them; approximate number and type of works and documents per artist; whether they are concentrated in a few cities or distributed across Europe. UFDA can organise an open call (own channels plus a targeted Meta campaign); paid outreach estimated at €2,500–6,000.
2. **Digitisation logistics** — described as the largest cost. Her model assumes photographer fees, equipment transport, travel, accommodation, per diems, local coordination, insurance, post-production and metadata work. Suggests regional digitisation sessions in cities with several selected artists, using local photographers or artists' existing high-quality files for remote locations. Phased plan: outreach/selection → short mapping phase (locations, archive size, available documentation, technical requirements) → final production and travel plan → digitisation, metadata, artist review, publication.
3. **Archive infrastructure (critical)** — UFDA must retain control of the digitised master files and the core archive infrastructure. Open to integrating GARF's registry, permanent identifiers and public-facing layer, but the arrangement must not transfer sole storage or control of archive materials to a third-party platform. To be reflected in the partnership agreement, artist consent process, access rights, exports and data protection. UFDA offers legal/workflow support on consents, image rights and use permissions.
4. **Roles** — GARF: lead coordination of grant application and reporting, international visibility, media and institutional outreach, identifier/registry layer, dissemination. UFDA: outreach, production expertise, digitisation, artist support.
5. **Independence of the Ukrainian project** — asks whether GARF has any current or previous partnerships, funding relationships or public programmes involving Russian organisations, especially state-affiliated, sanctioned or politically connected ones; needs confirmation that no such entity would participate in, access, benefit from or be publicly associated with the Ukrainian project. Requires clearly separate identity, Ukrainian-led participant selection, distinct communications.

**Jan's answer (2026-10-13):** GARF has never had any projects with any Russian organisations. Confirmed clean — no partnerships, funding relationships or public programmes involving Russian entities. The independence condition can therefore be answered plainly.

**Jan's position on photographers (2026-10-13):** he does not agree that professional photographers need to be involved for the photo documentation — the photographer-fee cost line in UFDA's digitisation model is not accepted as a requirement. For identification of artworks it is important that artists are also able to take proper photographs themselves for this kind of documentation, if artists are going to take control of their documentation. See mem://features/artist-photo-documentation.

**Jan's terms for the cooperation (2026-10-13):**
- If UFDA needs high-resolution professional photography, UFDA must also pay that cost — it is not a GARF requirement.
- GARF is in charge of storing the artists' archives, including the photographs. Each archive is the artist's property, including the photographs.
- GARF's task is to make the IT solution and help artists document their artworks in a proper way.
- UFDA may have their own agreement with the artists, but the cooperation is not possible if the artist–UFDA arrangement does not fit GARF's terms of use between GARF and the artists.

**Jan's concern (2026-10-13):** point 3 is not clear enough to accept as written. Her sentence only rules out sole storage/control moving to a third-party platform — it does not say whether an artist may also place the full master set into their own GARF archive, what happens to the masters if UFDA can no longer continue, or who the artist's counterpart is for access, correction, deletion and heir handover. The brief already stated that GARF provides the archive platform and that the outputs are 15 artist-owned archives with named heirs (mem://features/ufda-collaboration open points below), so agreement wording must not quietly reduce GARF to a public layer over an archive UFDA holds.

Open points before UFDA assesses the implementation model: existing artist pool, intended geography, archive volume, proposed budget share for UFDA. Jan decides how and when to reply — no draft unless he asks.
