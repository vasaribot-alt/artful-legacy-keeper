Ambassador asks (Jessica Sjöholm Skrubbe, Mick Wilson, Sonia Boyce and any later ambassador letters):

- **Never introduce GARF** in an ambassador letter. No mission pitch, no "I am writing to introduce myself and the foundation", no description of the registry, no re-sending of the presentation links. The recipient has already been — or will be — introduced in a separate personal letter from Jan.
- The ask is framed as Jan's own: **when you have had a chance to look into what we are doing, it would be nice if you felt that it is something you would like to support, and that you could see yourself as an ambassador for it.** Keep his phrasing, including "it would be nice if you felt".
- What the ask involves, stated plainly: no workload and no obligation — simply lending your name as a supporter.
- Caroline McCormick may be named as the adviser behind the ambassador idea (Chair of a UK foundation's Board of Trustees, Director of Achates).
- One page each, English, signed "Warm regards, Jan S Kindem" + foundation + email + website.
- Reference the earlier contact (Malmö conversation, the letter Jan already sent) rather than explaining GARF again.
