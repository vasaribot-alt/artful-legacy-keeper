---
name: IACCCA collector outreach
description: Collector pitch mirrors museum ask but access is scoped to their own collected artists who registered — requires new collection-scoped permission model
type: feature
---
IACCCA collectors get almost the museum approach: ask for names of contemporary artists in their collection, rough work counts per artist, and contact details to invite them. Difference from museums: collectors do NOT get access to the entire archive — only to the archives of artists in their own collection who have registered and built an archive. This scoped access is a new permission model (collector access tied to collection contents) that must be designed and built before any IACCCA outreach is sent. Consent guardrail unchanged: shared data only identifies/invites artists, never published, never placed in a profile without the artist's own action.
