The Creative Europe (European Cooperation Projects, medium scale) consortium is scoped to a Nordic–Baltic cluster: Netherlands (coordinator GARF + RKD standards partner), Norway (Mimeta), Sweden, Denmark, Finland, and the three Baltic states (Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania).

- Norway is eligible via EEA participation, not EU membership.
- All eight countries are eligible; comfortably exceeds the 5-country medium-scale minimum.
- Open decision: all three Baltic states as funded partners, or one leads with the other two as associated partners (settles during Sep–Oct 2026 partner conversations).
- Budget scaled to €1.4M eligible / €980K EU (70%, under €1M cap) / €420K own contribution, matched by partner staff time + DOEN Dutch application + own funds.
- .ART registry (UK) joins as associated partner only — UK is outside the programme.
