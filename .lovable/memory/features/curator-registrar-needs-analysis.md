Jan wants to understand what GARF can offer curators and registrars before building. Desk research completed 2026-09-17; deliverable at Files: GARF_Curator_and_Registrar_Needs_Analysis.md.

Key findings: registrars suffer manual re-typing of object data (ICOM-documented), CMS vendor lock-in, and freelancers lack an open verified directory; curators lose hours to inconsistent artist CVs, bad images/captions, 12-18 month paper loan workflows, and network-only artist discovery. Both groups share long-term duty of care as ethical anchor — the 100-year promise resonates.

Positioning rule: GARF is the permanent neutral layer UNDER collection management systems (TMS/EMu/MuseumPlus), never a competitor to them.

Ranked feature proposals: R1 canonical portable record, R2 registrar directory (built), R3 registrar CRM, R4 condition report templates, R5 provenance ledger, R6 estate toolkit (built), R7 CMS export connectors; C1 canonical CV, C2 CR-grade schema (built), C3 institutional attestations, C4 rights-cleared images, C5 loan-readiness packet PDF, C6 curator discovery search, C7 citation export, C8 willing-to-lend search.

Validation step planned: interviews with registrars/curators in network before building. Open questions: who pays for registrar tools, institutional liability for attestations, first export format (LIDO/CSV/EODEM).
