name: Creative Europe institutional partner candidates
description: Museum, academy and university candidates per country for the Creative Europe consortium
type: feature
---
Each consortium country pairs an artists' association with at least one museum, academy or university. Institutions can join as associated partners (letter of support + hosting) rather than funded beneficiaries.

- Sweden: Lund University (existing contact); alternatives Fotografiska Stockholm, Gothenburg Museum of Art.
- Norway: University of Oslo (existing contact); alternatives Haugar Art Museum, Henie Onstad Kunstsenter.
- Denmark: Louisiana Museum of Modern Art or ARoS Aarhus (targets).
- Finland: Finnish National Gallery or Aalto University (targets).
- Netherlands: RKD (core standards partner).
- Baltics: Estonian Academy of Arts, Art Academy of Latvia, Vilnius Academy of Arts (targets).
