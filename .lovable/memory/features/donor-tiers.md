Supporter recognition tiers (public /donors page, managed via DonorManager):
- Bronze €1,000, Silver €2,500, Gold €5,000, Platinum €10,000 and above.

Corporate-collections outreach ask (generate-outreach-email): €5 per contemporary work as a guideline; large collections choose a recognition tier instead of counting every work. Alternatives: institutional endorsement or a small pilot. Never include IBAN in cold emails; direct to /why-garf-matters and /donate (bank transfer shown there until Stripe cards arrive).
