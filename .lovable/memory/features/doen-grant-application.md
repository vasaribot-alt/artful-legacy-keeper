Applying to Stichting DOEN (Dutch lottery-funded grantmaker). Key constraints:
- DOEN excludes: preservation/acquisition of cultural heritage & artworks, standalone websites/publications, awareness campaigns, conferences, commercial objectives. GARF must be framed as a **participation/capacity-building programme for living artists**, never as heritage preservation or tech development.
- GEOGRAPHIC ELIGIBILITY (critical): Themes are region-restricted:
  - Regenerative economy → Netherlands and East Africa only
  - Transformative Power of Imagination → East Africa and SWANA region (Southwest Asia and North Africa) only
  - Solidarity society → (check current DOEN site for regions)
  - GARF is a Dutch stichting. A Netherlands-based org can apply under Regenerative economy (NL eligible), but NOT under Transformative Power of Imagination (NL not in eligible regions). The "Every Artist a Legacy" proposal as drafted (Dutch/European artists) was framed under Transformative Power of Imagination — NOT eligible unless project activities run in East Africa or SWANA.
- Catalytic effect valued. Legal entity required (GARF is a stichting — OK). Applications ≥4 months before start, via DOEN form.
- Project drafted (Sept 2026): "Every Artist a Legacy", 24 months, €150k requested of €212k total; 500 artists onboarded, 12 registrar fellows, succession service, open registry. Doc: /mnt/documents/DOEN_GARF_Project_Proposal.md
- STATUS (Sept 2026): User chose option (b) — proposal pivoted to East Africa & SWANA under Transformative Power of Imagination (v2). All programme activities now run in the regions: 12 regional registrar fellows (Kenya/Uganda/Tanzania/Ethiopia/Egypt/Jordan/Lebanon/Morocco), 500 regional artists, EN/AR/FR/SW localisation, regional partner grants (€36k). GARF remains the Dutch applicant/legal entity; platform, training and data stewardship from Amsterdam. Docs: /mnt/documents/DOEN_GARF_Project_Proposal.md + .docx (v2).
