IFAR (International Foundation for Art Research) is closing, with archives transferring to another party. Jan spoke directly with the IFAR leader, who agreed that GARF may position itself as "working in the spirit of IFAR" — supporting contemporary artists in preserving their documentation for future generations.

Key points:
- IFAR is winding down; no active partner to negotiate with, but also no one to deny the claim.
- GARF did NOT acquire IFAR or its archives. GARF is an independent Dutch stichting continuing a similar mission for living/contemporary artists.
- GARF does NOT produce catalogue raisonnés. Living artists compile their own; committees or foundations handle deceased artists. GARF provides infrastructure and long-term hosting.
- The CR Public Directory and scholarly pages are IFAR-style in spirit — a public scholarly reference, not a claim of succession.
- Use the phrase "in the spirit of IFAR" when positioning GARF's preservation mission to museums, researchers, and scholarly audiences.
