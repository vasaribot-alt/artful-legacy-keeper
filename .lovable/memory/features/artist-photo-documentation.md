---
name: Artist photo documentation
description: Professional photographers are not a requirement for identification-grade artwork documentation — artists must be able to photograph their own works properly
type: constraint
---

Jan's position (2026-10-13): professional photographers must not be treated as necessary for photo documentation. For identification of artworks it is important that artists are also able to take proper photographs themselves for this kind of documentation — if artists are going to take control of their documentation.

Implications:
- Project budgets and workflows (UFDA / Digital Original pilot, DOEN proposal, any institutional digitisation plan) must not make paid photographer sessions the default, and must not gate artwork identification or registration on professional photography.
- GARF's task is to make artist-taken documentation good enough: capture guidance, simple quality feedback, and upload help — so the artist, not a hired crew, is the one securing the record.
- Professional photography remains an optional route for publication-grade or conservation-grade images (and stays a registrar work area), never a precondition for having a work identified, catalogued or preserved.
