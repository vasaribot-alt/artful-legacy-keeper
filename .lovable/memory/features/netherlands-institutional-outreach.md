Alongside the Norwegian batch, a deliberately small Dutch batch supports the ANBI application in the Netherlands by building visible Dutch institutional relationships.

- Same three-point exchange pitch as Norway: ask for their documentation on contemporary artists, offer free verified access to GARF archives, state plainly that nothing enters an artist's archive without that artist's consent and source material is used only to identify and invite the artist (orphan artworks flow).
- Batch tag `netherlands_institutions_2026` in `alliance_outreach_targets`: 11 museums/institutions (Stedelijk, Van Abbemuseum, Boijmans, Kröller-Müller, Bonnefanten, Kunstmuseum Den Haag, De Pont, Centraal Museum, Rijksakademie, West Den Haag, Stroom Den Haag) and 8 contemporary galleries (GRIMM, Fons Welters, Annet Gelink, Ron Mandos, tegenboschvanvreden, Upstream, Stigter Van Doesburg, Dürst Britt & Mayhew).
- Stroom Den Haag added 2026-10-03 as a partnership target (verified public contact hknl@stroom.nl from thehagueartists.nl/information). Pitch is partnership, not rescue: their Hague Artists database is a discovery tool with no preservation guarantee — "let your artists' work outlive the platform". Natural first Dutch partner; a Stroom endorsement could open the rest of the Dutch batch.
- Only publicly listed email addresses are used; addresses are never guessed.
- West Den Haag is a non-profit exhibition space (formerly Galerie West), not a commercial gallery. Mondriaan Fonds is deliberately excluded from this batch: it is a funder, not a collection holder.
- Keep the Dutch batch small and slow; send in reviewed batches only, and prepare a Dutch-language version of the pitch before sending.
